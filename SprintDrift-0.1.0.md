# Sprint Drift 0.1.0

Sprint Drift is a small Mac companion for keeping an intention in sight while
you work, capturing what happens along the way, and ending with a record you
can review.

This first public preview includes:

- timed sprints with a compact floating companion;
- typed notes, parked sparks, pause and return context, overtime, and recovery
  for an interrupted sprint;
- an optional 227.5 MB voice model that downloads only when you choose it and
  transcribes recordings on this Mac;
- a chronological review trail with editable corrections and an optional
  reflection;
- searchable local sprint records and a chosen-folder mirror; and
- signed update checks with confirmation before an update is installed.

## Requirements and limits

- macOS 15 or later on Apple silicon;
- no account, built-in sync, analytics, or cloud backup;
- the canonical sprint record stays locally accessible on your Mac;
- optional voice capture requires the separate model download; and
- this is an early public preview, so please keep your own backup of anything
  important.

For help or a bug report, use
[GitHub Issues](https://github.com/jerrytmcl/Sprint-Drift-Downloads/issues). Please do not
include private intentions, notes, transcripts, recordings, credentials, or
unsanitized screenshots in a public issue.
